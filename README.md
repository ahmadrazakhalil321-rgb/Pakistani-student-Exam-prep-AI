# PakExam AI Prep 🇵🇰📚

An AI-powered study companion designed for Pakistani Matric and Intermediate (FBISE & Boards) students. Upload textbook chapters, generate board-style practice questions, evaluate your answers, and get explanations in **English, Urdu, or Roman Urdu**.

## Features
- **PDF/Text Chapter Upload:** Supports your standard board textbooks.
- **Board-Style Question Generator:** Creates MCQs, Short Questions, and Long Questions.
- **Smart Answer Evaluation:** Checks student answers against the uploaded textbook text.
- **Multilingual Support:** Explanations and feedback in English, Urdu (اردو), or Roman Urdu.
